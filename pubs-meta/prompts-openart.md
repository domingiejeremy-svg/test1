# Prompts OpenArt — Calendrier de l'Avent Padel Occas

## 1. Vidéo de pub (image → vidéo) — le meilleur usage de tes crédits

Dans OpenArt, choisis **Image to Video**, importe `source/calendrier.jpg` (ta photo du calendrier), format **9:16** si proposé, durée **5 à 8 secondes**.

Prompt :

> Slow cinematic push-in on a handmade advent calendar hanging on a light wall: 24 kraft paper bags with orange padel-ball stickers numbered 1 to 24, held by wooden pegs on jute strings. Warm golden fairy lights gently twinkle above the calendar, soft snowflakes drift slowly in front of the camera, a pine branch in the bottom corner sways slightly. Cozy Christmas evening atmosphere, warm soft light, shallow depth of field. The bags and numbers stay perfectly still and sharp. No people, no hands, no text, no logos.

Si l'outil a un champ « negative prompt » :

> distorted numbers, changing numbers, melting bags, extra bags, hands, people, text, logo, flicker, blur on the calendar

**À vérifier** : les numéros ne doivent pas se déformer pendant la vidéo. Si ça arrive, relance avec un mouvement plus faible (« very subtle camera movement »).

## 2. Image d'ambiance de Noël (pour remplacer la photo sur fond gris)

Choisis **Text to Image** ou **Image to Image** avec ta photo, format **4:5**.

> Realistic lifestyle product photo, cozy Christmas living room in the evening. A handmade advent calendar hangs on a warm beige wall: 24 kraft paper bags with folded tops, held by light wooden clothespins on three jute strings, 8 bags per string. Each bag has a round orange padel-ball sticker (#FC9A20) with curved white seams and a bold navy number (#060A2A), numbers 1 to 24, each exactly once. Next to it, a decorated Christmas tree with warm golden fairy lights, soft bokeh, a padel racket leaning against a wooden sideboard below the calendar. Warm, soft, inviting light, high detail, sharp numbers. No people, no brand logos, no other text.

## Ensuite

Envoie-moi le résultat (vidéo ou image) : je le vérifie, puis je l'intègre aux pubs et à la page du calendrier.
